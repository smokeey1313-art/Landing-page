Questions

The questions a professional asks.

Will marketplaces flag my AI‑generated content?

Only if it's mislabeled. WizSuite attaches the disclosure paperwork required by each marketplace to every export. You publish under your own account, as the publisher of record.

How is solvability guaranteed?

Our Clue‑Truth Proof re‑solves every puzzle from its printed clues before export; anything ambiguous is destroyed at generation. In the live console, "Prove it solves" runs that same check on the puzzle in front of you.

How real is the localization?

Clues, stories and instructions are re‑authored per language — not machine‑pasted. Arabic and Urdu get full right‑to‑left typesetting with ligature‑safe grids, Hindi gets Devanagari shaping, and European languages get accent‑aware word grids (Ä, Ö, É, Ñ all place correctly).

What keeps my catalog from looking generated?

Nothing is templated. Every grid, plate and palette is computed per book and hashed by Duplicate‑Guard against your catalog and the public pool — your readers never see the same puzzle twice, and neither does marketplace dedupe.

Can I bring my own stories from Claude or Google AI?

Yes. Paste any custom narrative text — detective cases, fantasy campaigns, true‑crime stories — into the engine console. WizSuite analyzes the text, maps the logic variables, and converts it into a playable grid with one verified solution.

What counts against my export allowance?

One finished book package: interior PDF or PNG plus cover wrap. Failed spec validations re‑run free, and correction re‑exports within 30 days are free. Drafts and previews are always unlimited.

Who owns the output and the royalties?

You do, entirely — full commercial rights on your own marketplace account. WizSuite never writes to your account and never touches your money.

Can I wire it into my own pipeline?

Studio includes the engine API and webhooks: send a niche and language, receive print‑ready PDF, cover wrap and metadata JSON.
